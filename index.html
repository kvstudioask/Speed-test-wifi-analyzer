1. Introduction

KVCreation (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Android application Speed Test – WiFi Analyzer (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is handled, and your choices. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • Speed and network results are processed to show you diagnostics and history on your device.
  • Location permission is requested because Android requires it for Wi‑Fi scanning and related network details—not to track you across apps.
  • The App may show ads (including rewarded ads). Ad partners such as Google AdMob may collect device and usage data under their own policies.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, the App may process:

  • Network performance data — download/upload throughput, latency, jitter, packet loss estimates, DNS timings, traceroute hops, and similar measurement results.
  • Wi‑Fi / local network details — SSID, BSSID, signal strength, frequency, channel, link speed, and nearby access-point scan results (when you grant permission).
  • Approximate location / region signals — used where needed for Wi‑Fi APIs, regulatory channel context, or selecting nearby test infrastructure. Precise continuous background tracking is not the purpose of the App.
  • Device & app settings — theme, vibration preference, selected test server, temporary ad-free status, and tool unlock state stored locally on your device.
  • History & exports you create — past test results kept on-device until you clear them; files you export or share leave the device only when you choose to share them.
  • Advertising identifiers & ad interaction data — processed by our advertising SDK partners when ads are enabled (see Section 5).

4. How we use information

  • To run speed tests, Wi‑Fi analysis, gaming/streaming suitability checks, and diagnostics.
  • To display results, history, tips, and quality ratings inside the App.
  • To remember preferences such as theme, server selection, and temporary ad-free rewards.
  • To show advertisements that help keep the App free, and to honor rewarded “ad-free” periods.
  • To improve reliability (for example retries, server selection, and crash-safe measurement flow).

5. Third-party services

The App may communicate with third parties to provide core features:

  • Measurement / test servers — when you run a test, traffic is sent to the selected or auto-chosen speed-test endpoint so throughput and latency can be measured.
  • Public network utilities — features such as IP check, DNS lookup, website timing, or traceroute may contact public hosts or resolvers you select or that the feature uses.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — for banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google may collect and process data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance.

We do not control third-party sites or SDKs beyond configuring the App to use them. Their privacy policies govern their processing.

6. Permissions

On Android, the App may request:

  • Internet / network state — to run tests and load ads.
  • Wi‑Fi state — to read connection details and assist scanning features.
  • Location (approximate / precise) — required by Android for Wi‑Fi scan results and related APIs.
  • Notifications — for optional monitoring or status alerts you enable.
  • Vibration — for optional haptic feedback.

You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings; some features will then be limited or unavailable.

7. Data storage and retention

  • Most preferences and history are stored locally on your device.
  • Uninstalling the App typically removes local App data.
  • Data sent to test servers or ad networks is retained according to those providers’ policies.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Your choices

  • Limit or revoke location and other permissions in Android settings.
  • Use in-app options such as temporary ad-free rewards where available.
  • Reset advertising ID in your device settings (Android advertising preferences).
  • Clear App storage or uninstall the App to remove local history and preferences.

10. International users

Measurement and advertising partners may process data on servers outside your country. If you use the App, you understand that such transfers may occur as needed to provide the service.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
